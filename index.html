About this TV

The BRAVIA 9 II is Sony's 2026 True RGB 4K HDR Google TV series. It uses independently controlled red, green and blue LEDs, local dimming and Sony's XR processing to target high brightness, accurate colour and detailed contrast.

Model note: Exact model suffixes can vary by market. This guide uses the 65-inch BRAVIA 9 II specification family, such as K-65XR90M2 / K-65XR90SM2. Always check the model label on your TV before using model-specific accessories or service instructions.

Display and Picture Quality

True RGB technology uses separately controlled red, green and blue LEDs to improve colour volume and colour reproduction. X-Wide Angle Pro is designed to help maintain colour and contrast from different viewing positions.

The TV also includes RGB Triluminos Max, Luminance Booster Pro, XR Contrast Booster 40, XR Clear Image, local dimming and Immersive Black Screen Pro. Supported HDR formats include HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision.

Sound

The 65-inch specification lists 80 W audio output with Acoustic Multi-Audio+, multiple speaker units and sound-positioning/beam tweeters. Dolby Atmos and DTS:X are supported, and Acoustic Auto Calibration can compensate for room and listening-position conditions.

Gaming Features

  • 120 Hz native panel refresh rate.
  • 4K120 input support on compatible HDMI connections.
  • VRR support on HDMI 3 and 4.
  • ALLM support on HDMI 1-4.
  • Auto HDR Tone Mapping and Auto Genre Picture Mode for compatible PlayStation 5 use.
  • Dedicated FPS and RTS game picture modes are available in the supported specification.

Important Correction from the Earlier Version

The previous page listed XBR-65A9G as the BRAVIA 9 II model. That was incorrect. XBR-65A9G belongs to Sony's 2019 A9G MASTER Series OLED range. BRAVIA 9 II is a separate 2026 True RGB LCD/RGB LED product family.
