BRAVIA 9 II Features

A detailed overview of picture, sound, smart TV, connectivity, gaming and convenience features.

Picture Features

True RGB

Independently controlled red, green and blue LEDs are used to expand colour range and improve colour reproduction.

X-Wide Angle Pro

Designed to preserve colour and contrast when the TV is viewed from different seating positions.

RGB Triluminos Max

Works with Sony's colour processing to maintain rich and accurate colour reproduction.

Luminance Booster Pro

Helps increase peak brightness while maintaining colour performance.

Immersive Black Screen Pro

Low-reflection treatment helps reduce distracting reflections and supports better shadow visibility.

XR Contrast Booster 40

Works with local dimming and XR processing to improve contrast between bright and dark areas.

XR Clear Image

Picture processing is designed to improve clarity and detail from compatible content.

HDR Support

Supports HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision formats, depending on content and service compatibility.

Studio Calibrated

Supported calibrated modes include Netflix, SONY PICTURES CORE and Amazon Prime.

Sound Features

  • 80 W audio output in the 65-inch specification.
  • Acoustic Multi-Audio+ with multiple speaker units and sound-positioning/beam tweeters.
  • Dolby Atmos and DTS:X support.
  • Acoustic Auto Calibration for room and listening-position compensation.
  • Voice Zoom 3 for clearer dialogue with supported configurations.
  • Acoustic Center Sync with compatible Sony soundbars.

Smart TV Features

Feature Purpose

Apple AirPlay Stream compatible content from supported Apple devices.
Apple HomeKit Supports compatible smart-home control functions.
BRAVIA Sync Uses HDMI-CEC for compatible device control.
32 GB storage Provides onboard storage for the TV system and supported apps.

Gaming and HDMI 2.1

4K at 120 Hz

Compatible HDMI sources can provide 4K120 input.

VRR

Variable Refresh Rate is supported on HDMI 3 and 4 in the cited 65-inch specification.

ALLM

Auto Low Latency Mode is supported on HDMI 1–4.

eARC

Supports HDMI audio return for compatible audio equipment.

PS5 Features

Auto HDR Tone Mapping and Auto Genre Picture Mode are supported for compatible PlayStation 5 use.

Game Modes

Supported picture options include FPS game and RTS game modes.

Wireless and Physical Connections

Connection Details
Wi-Fi Wi-Fi 6E, with 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz and 6 GHz frequency support in the cited specification.
Bluetooth Bluetooth 5.3 with supported profiles for compatible devices.

USB 2 side USB ports in the cited specification.
LAN 1 Ethernet/LAN input.
